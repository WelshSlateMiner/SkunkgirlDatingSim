During normal encounters, outside of dates, a skunkgirl can choose to leave or need to leave at any time. This is based on their profession, their schedule and/or interest in the conversation. When a skunkgirl leaves the scene, the player enters Solo mode. Following a skunkgirl to her next location may be taken as creepy, especially if done more than once in a row. The skunkgirls don’t know that the player can see their location.

Base the visual effects on what it would realistically look like if a skunk really sprayed someone who was wearing goggles or a camera lens.

Most intimate farts are large. Base this on the sound effect used for each specific fart. Assign them a value of 1-3. The gas layer starts at the very bottom of the screen and very slowly fills the screen from bottom to top. A small fart adds a little bit of gas, a large fart adds more.
