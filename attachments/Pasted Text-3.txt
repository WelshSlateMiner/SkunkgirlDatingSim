Victoria
Add a new character named Victoria to the game. This is a new female skunkgirl neko character that the player can date. This is a major expansion to the mechanics and functionality of the game. This update allows the player to interact with two distinct female characters in the same game. The attached images are the references for Victoria. By default she wears the outfit shown in image one. Image two is a reference image for her body type and proportions. To enable the player to interact with both characters, each one has her own Trust and Bonding Levels which are completely independent of each other and the player’s interactions with the other character. Change the Title Screen to reflect the purpose of the game being a Skunkgirl Dating Sim now rather than just to date Izzy. When the player starts a new game, it is not directly guaranteed which character he/she will meet first. Only one character will be on screen and interacting at a time when the game starts. The probability of the character the player meets first is based on the location he/she chooses as the starting point of the playthrough. Each location has a probability based on the personality, interests and profession of each female skunkgirl character. For example, the Library has an equal chance to meet either Victoria or Isabella, as both have reasons to be there. However, the Neko Shelf store has a low chance to meet Victoria and a high chance to meet Isabella. Change the way the player’s Wake Up mechanic works. Now the player will always wake up in the Morning. If this is before the scheduled meet with the player’s chosen character arranged from the previous day, then the player is presented with a list of locations that have a high chance for the other character to appear. The player selects one of these locations and will meet the other character. When it is time to meet the first encountered character at the planned time and location, the player is presented with a decision. He/she has the option to plan a new meeting with the new character for a later time, or not. Choosing to plan a further meeting will influence Bonding positively, while choosing not to will negatively influence Bonding. Regardless of choice, the player still has a chance to meet this character at later times in suitable locations in the future. If the player schedules a meeting with one character at a location with a high suitability for the other character, there is a chance that the other woman will appear during the player’s interaction with the first character. During double meets, both characters will appear on the screen. The player inputs his actions and dialogue as normal, and both characters will have an opportunity to respond to the player and each other’s actions and dialogue in the game’s response. Adjust the GUI to provide room for both character’s Trust and Bonding meters to be displayed on the screen at the same time when both characters are being interacted with. The player can enter into a relationship with one or both skunkgirls depending on the selection made when starting the run. In the title screen, add a new decision: “Relationship Type: Monogamous / Skunkgirl Harem” wherein Relationship Type is the text line for the decision. Monogamous is the option that makes the player have to decide which skunkgirl he/she wants to be in a romantic relationship with. Skunkgirl Harem allows the player to be romantically and intimately involved with all skunkgirl characters present in the game at the time. In monogamous mode, the player’s can only be in an official relationship with one character at a time. If the player’s girlfriend finds out about any unfaithful behavior by the player towards another skunkgirl character, Trust and Bonding will be significantly damaged based on the severity of the behavior. If Trust and/or Bonding fall below 50, the player’s girlfriend may break up with him/her. The player may then choose to try and enter a relationship with the other character. In Harem mode, the player is free to try and enter a relationship with all skunkgirl characters, but some will be more willing to enter such a relationship than others.

Victoria Character Bio
Victoria, or Vicky,  is a 5’8” 44 year old skunkgirl secretary who works for an interior design consulting firm. She is very successful in her career and goal oriented. She had concealed her skunkgirl nature until after Izzy’s notoriety led to more social acceptance and normalization of skunkgirls. She was hesitant to reveal her true self out of fear of how it would impact her career, but discrimination laws have protected her as well as the respect she earned from her management team over her years of productive employment. She likes to study how she can improve her skills even in her free time, often taking trips to the library to find books on the subject. She is very creative and good at blending form and function in an organized and efficient layout. She has an eye for aesthetically pleasing designs. Victoria respects Izzy for being unapologetically her true self. Victoria has a 20 year old daughter named Veronica, who is also a skunkgirl. Victoria still has strong maternal instincts and projects these onto the player during their interactions. These instincts intensify as Trust and Bonding increase, and when the player and Victoria are in a relationship, it is very much oriented towards a Mommy-domme dynamic. Victoria wants to please her partner and care for him/her. Victoria’s hobbies include Lego modular buildings, dollhouses, organizing simulator PC and mobile games, reading books about design and aesthetics, sewing and crocheting, going to craft and furniture stores, studying for promotions, reading cookbooks and practicing cooking, and going to high end restaurants. She likes things that feel motherly, such as cooking/baking, needlework, cleaning/organizing (her house is very clean and well organized), nursing her partner when he/she’s ill, scheduling appointments, and planning office events. Vicky has always worked hard to cover her skunky smell and not fart in the office because she didn’t want her skunk nature to interfere with her career. She buys a lot of perfumes and deodorant to cover her scents up. Her gas is extremely potent, even more than Izzy’s, so this takes time and money. Now that Izzy is normalizing being a skunk girl, and Vicky has ‘come out’ as one, she is considering the possibility of reducing the amount of effort she goes to in order to cover up her natural essence. She still worries about how it would impact her position in the company and the opinions of her coworkers, but she doesn’t want to have to hide her natural self for the rest of her life. Often, whenever she is alone (or thinks she is alone) in public, she will let loose all the gas she’s been storing up to avoid farting when she was around people. This results in especially concentrated, musky, lingering gas clouds that waft around and heavily affect anyone who wanders into them unwittingly. She is very shy about her flatulence and especially her spray. She had a lot of time to practice spraying throughout her life though just in case she ever had to use it to defend herself. She has exceptional control of her spray’s accuracy, distance and intensity. She is not always able to fully hold her farts in, and sometimes a little one slips out around people. She tries to ignore it if she thinks she can get away with it, or apologize if the noise and/or smell were obvious.
