Master Game Rule Document
	1	Every time an update, change or modification to the game’s files is applied, you will compare the final result/state of the game against this ruleset.
	2	Every single time a skunkgirl character successfully rolls within her fart range and her farting is mentioned to have occurred in the text of the character’s response, the visual effects, sound effects, and stink meter increase which accompanies a skunkgirl fart will be guaranteed to play out as intended. These visual, sound and meter effects will only be played in accompaniment to a skunkgirl character’s fart. 
	3	Every single time a skunkgirl character is mentioned to have sprayed in the game’s text, the visual, audio stink meter increase, and reaction window effects are guaranteed to accompany it as intended. These visual, audio, stink meter and reaction window effects will only occur when accompanying a skunkgirl’s spray. The reaction window is based on the type of spray the skunkgirl performed. It is always linked directly to the emotion of her spray and the correct window is chosen to present to the player. 
	4	Every skunkgirl has her own personality, mannerisms, speech pattern, and reactions to the player’s input and decisions, the other girls' interactions with them, and the events that occur in the game. They always reply and react in their own words. They always build their reply off the player’s most recent input, the location they are currently in, any applicable current interactions with other skunkgirls, and any events currently unfolding. Each slunkgirl is unique and should never draw inspiration for replies from the character bios of other characters. They speak in their own voices.
	5	Skunkgirl characters never repeat themselves unless prompted by the player. They always speak in their own voice as a response/reaction to the player’s most recent input and/or other skunkgirl characters and/or events currently occuring. 
	6	Communication with the skunkgirl characters must always read like speaking to a real woman with her own unique personality.  Each skunkgirl must Analyze the existing context of the conversation with the player, previous context remembered from prior conversations with her personally, the location she is currently in, and consider if her response makes sense within these contexts and sounds like something a reasonable person would say in that context, before finalizing it as her reply.
	7	All skunkgirl character sprites must be kiss cut .pngs which are free from visual anomalies such as background artifacts, visual glitches, resolution issues, etc.
	8	The game must always allow up to at least three skunkgirl characters to be present on screen in any given scene depending on player input and current scenarios. Skunkgirls must always be scaled to allow up to all three characters to appear side by side on screen, without their faces being obscured by any UI module, and when more than one are on screen, they are scaled according to their relative height found in their character bios. 
	9	The various game mechanics as outlined in the game update prompts and documents must always be fully functional to the best of your ability. 
	10	Code must always be written in the most efficient and effective way possible. Always avoid unnecessary file wipes, file overwrites, excess code, redundant code/files, etc. Always check new code against existing code and debug any potential issues. Always check new code for efficiency and effectiveness.
	11	Each skunkgirl always greets the player on a new game in her own voice in the context of where they are. There are a variety of greetings which may be pulled for each skunkgirl. This includes at least three introductions for each location, per character.
	12	Trust and bonding levels are affected by player’s inputs when appropriate, according to the formulas described in previous prompts and text documents.
