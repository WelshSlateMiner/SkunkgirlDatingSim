Add a new gamemode selection feature to the title screen. The text of the selection line reads “Gamemode:” and the options are Slow Romance / Casual
Option 1, Slow Romance, reduces the gains earned towards Trust and Bonding levels. This game mode allows for more realistic, natural romance between the player and Izzy. It also introduces a tally of dates the player has taken Izzy on. The player can invite Izzy on a date after reaching 25 Trust and Bonding. After reaching 25 in each category, the player can only increase those stats up to 50 after he/she has taken Izzy on a date. To increase the stats past 50 up to 75, the player must take Izzy on a second date. To increase beyond 75 up to 100, and to enable the possibility of becoming a couple with Izzy, the player must take Izzy on a third date. The Date Tally keeps track of these dates, reading “Dates: X/3” where X is the number of dates the player has had with Izzy, from 0-3. Izzy is more likely to enter a relationship with the player based on the locations the player took her on dates to. Dates can be spontaneous or they can be planned in advance. If the player plans a date for the next day with Izzy, including time of day and location, then that will be the next meeting point for the next day when the player wakes up from sleep.

Gamemode Option 2 is Casual. This gamemode is the current default state of the game. There are no changes to be made to this gamemode at this time.
