Implement an event system. It’s sort of like an internal Dungeon Master, which only very rarely pops in with random events to make things more interesting. The events will fire on a probability system which is rolled each time the player makes an input when under eligible conditions, and it may be influenced by other factors. Each event is based around a specific incident occurring during the conversation between the player and a skunkgirl. When an event fires, the specific incident will be worked into the current roleplay by taking place after the player’s most recent input and before the skunkgirl’s response, so the skunkgirl can react both to the event and the player’s input. However, some events will warrant her full attention, in which case she will only briefly acknowledge the player’s input before focusing on the more important issue. When events occur, their content is written around any important player generated context contained in the player’s current interaction with the skunkgirl; such as they are both sitting down, the player and Ronnie are racing, or whatever context may be relevant to the scenario. It is written like prose in a book, and italicized like a skunkgirl player’s action, since it is describing an action occurring around the player and characters.
Here is the first event:
Event name: Wolf Attack!
Locations: Nature Trail
Chance to fire: 2% per input during Morning and Afternoon, 4% during Evening and Night
Media: Generate a .png image consistent with the game’s art style of a snarling wolf crouching and preparing to lunge. Also generate a .png image in the game’s artstyle of a whimpering wolf which has been sprayed and has skunk spray on its fur, primarily around its face, shoulders and forelegs. 
Contents: A wolf sneaks up on the player and slunkgirl(s) in the scene. The skunkgirl spots it before the player does. The text describes the skunkgirl spotting it and reacting. The snarling wolf will appear on the right hand side of the screen facing towards the skunkgirl(s) who get bumped to the left side of the screen. Each skunkgirl will react differently. Izzy will be worried and look to the player for help, probably cowering behind him/her. She will prepare a defensive spray. Vicky will be scared, but try to protect the player due to her maternal instincts. She will also prepare a defensive spray, and probably tell the player to get behind her. Ronnie will be nervous but not want to show it. She will try to be tough and prepare an Angry spray. The player has an opportunity to input their reaction, and any actions they take to try and protect themselves and the skunkgirl(s) from the wolf. Then the event continues and the skunkgirl(s) will spray the wolf if they feel the player was not able to ward it off successfully. Vicky is the most likely to spray the wolf, even if the player tries to ward it off, as she will prefer to dissuade the player from taking action and to spray the wolf herself so she can fulfill the maternal duty to protect her loved one. Izzy is next most likely to spray, as she is probably so scared she doesn’t even realize the player is trying to ward off the wolf and spray anyway, possibly accidentally spraying the player as well. Ronnie is third most likely to spray, and will let the player take action if he/she is trying to ward the wolf off so she can stay safe. Otherwise she will spray the wolf and not be very concerned about potentially accidentally spraying the player if he/she is in the wrong place at the wrong time. If a skunkgirl sprays, swap out the snarling wolf sprite for the sprayed wolf sprite, and after three seconds, slide it off the screen to the right and out of the scene. If the player accidentally got sprayed as well, play the full spray animation including on-screen splatter visual effects. If a skunkgirl sprays the wolf at all in this event, she will also accidentally spray the player. If the player ever accidentally gets sprayed, a unique Accidentally Skunked window appears, similar to the existing spray event windows. This window explains it was an unintentional, heat of the moment incident, and the player can now choose how to react. There are three button options: Be Disgusted, Be Understanding, Be Aroused. Selecting Be Disgusted will taken poorly by Vicky and Izzy if they did it and damage their trust and bonding by 5 each. Ronnie will just laugh, comment on how bad you smell, and basically say shit happens, better to be skunked than eaten, so suck it up, buttercup. It doesn’t affect trust or bonding with her. Choosing Be Understanding will go over really well with Izzy and Vicky, boosting trust and bonding by 10. Izzy is very apologetic and super embarrassed, so she is so grateful if the player is understanding. Vicky is very sorry she got you, but she’s proud she scared off the wolf and considers it worth it, and she promises to make it up to you. Ronnie will, again, laugh and comment about how bad the player smells, but she will also appreciate the player being understanding and not holding it against her. It boosts trust and bonding by 5 each for her. Selecting Be Aroused will trigger different reactions from each girl based on their current trust and bonding levels. With Izzy, if her levels are below 50, she will be kinda creeped out, affecting Trust and Bonding negatively but not wanting to make a big deal out of it because she feels bad about spraying you. She will not play into it yet. If her levels are over 50, she will be pleasantly surprised, and the adrenaline of the whole situation will have her more excited, so she’ll probably invite you back to her place while using the excuse of, you stink too bad and ought to go to her place and get washed up. She has no intention of cleaning the player off- quite the opposite. It boosts Trust and Bonding by 10 each. Vicky will find it hard to believe if Trust and Bonding are below 50. She will think you’re just pretending to get her to want to sleep with you- although, she will also think to herself how hard it would be to fake being into skunk spray. She won’t play into it yet, but it will pique her interest, and only reduce trust and bonding by 5 each. Ronnie, with trust and bonding below 50, will just make fun of you for being into it, laugh about how bad you smell, and brag about chasing off the wolf. She will make fun of how the player was too scared and now he/she’s so high on adrenaline that her bravery and skunk spray has you turned on. Her trust and bonding go up by 2 points. If her trust and bonding levels are over 50, she will meet your interest with bemused curiosity. She will still drive home how bad the player smells, and how weird he/she is for being into it. She will threaten to do a Dominant Spray, to see if the player is serious about it or just bluffing. If the player doesn’t change his/her mind and beg her not to, she will let loose, and trigger a Dominant Spray. Gameplay continues as normal from there.
